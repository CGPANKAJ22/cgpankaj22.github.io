हमारी सेवाएँ

Har seva ke zaroori documents. Seva par dabaiye, list khul jayegi.

Aadhaar Sevaआधार सेवा · Aadhaar Center

  • Aadhaar Mobile Number Update
    Documents:
    • Aadhaar Card
    • Mobile Number
  • Aadhaar Name Correction
    Documents:
    • Aadhaar Card
    • Proof of Identity (POI)
    • Marksheet
    • Marriage Certificate
    • Birth Certificate
  • Aadhaar Date of Birth Correction
    Documents:
    • Aadhaar Card
    • Birth Certificate
    • Marksheet (Central or State Board)
  • Aadhaar Biometric Update
    Documents:
    • Aadhaar Card

Banking & PANबैंकिंग और पैन · CSC & Banking

  • PAN Card
    Documents:
    • Aadhaar Card
    • Birth Certificate
  • Aadhaar Seeding in Bank Account (DBT / NPCI)
    Documents:
    • Bank Passbook
    • Aadhaar Card
    • Mobile Number

RTO Sevaलाइसेंस और वाहन · CSC & Banking

  • Learner License (LL)
    Documents:
    • Aadhaar Card (with linked mobile number)
    • Passport Size Photo
    • Signature
    • Age Proof (10th Marksheet) if full date of birth is not on Aadhaar
    • Above 40 years: Medical Form 1A
  • Driving License (DL)
    Documents:
    • Learner License (at least 30 days old, within 6 months)
    • Aadhaar Card
    • Vehicle for Driving Test
  • DL Renewal
    Documents:
    • Old DL
    • Aadhaar Card
    • Passport Size Photo
    • Signature
    • Above 40 years: Medical Form 1A
  • Duplicate DL / DL Address Change
    Documents:
    • DL Copy or DL Number
    • Aadhaar Card
    • If lost: FIR / Police Report
  • RC Transfer (Vehicle Ownership Change)
    Documents:
    • RC
    • Insurance
    • PUC
    • Form 29 and 30 (signed by both)
    • Aadhaar of Buyer and Seller
  • Check / Pay Challan
    Documents:
    • Vehicle Number or DL Number
